# The Rise of Serbia, v0.1.0

First test build for Europa Universalis V 1.4.6. English localization.

This package has passed static checks and scripted scenario evaluation. It has **not** been loaded in the EU5 executable. Treat it as a prototype for an initial in-game test, not a verified release. All released DLC may remain enabled, but all-DLC runtime compatibility has not been tested here.

## Installation

1. Extract the ZIP. Place the `rise_of_serbia` folder in your EU5 user mod directory, normally `Documents/Paradox Interactive/Europa Universalis V/mod/`.
2. The folder should contain `.metadata/metadata.json`, `in_game/`, and `main_menu/` directly. Avoid an extra nested `rise_of_serbia` folder.
3. Enable **The Rise of Serbia** in the game's mod manager. If it does not appear, create a local mod with the game's mod tools and copy this folder's contents into that local mod's directory, retaining the registration generated by the game.
4. Start a **new 1337 campaign as Serbia**. This version does not support installing into an existing save. Use English for the first test.
5. Advance through the first monthly situation check and open **The Rise of Serbia** in the Situations view.

Do not copy files into the game's installation directory. Do not combine this build with another mod editing Serbian historical events or the Curse of Stefan Uroš III disaster.

## What is included

- Two linked situations: **The Rise of Serbia** during Dušan's reign and **The Emperor's Inheritance** after he ceases to rule Serbia. His death is not scripted or forced.
- Four expandable conquest cards: Macedonia, Albania, Epirus and Acarnania, and Thessaly.
- An expanded version of the existing ambitions event. It grants 20-year province conquest opportunities for the four objective groups. No free cores, automatic annexations, or forced wars.
- The existing imperial proclamation and patriarchate event, accessible through a situation action. Peace, direct ownership of Skopje, and the Macedonian objective are required. The later western and southern conquests are not coronation prerequisites.
- The existing imperial Code event and policy, including its costs and estate opposition, accessible through a situation action.
- Royal or delegated political settlements for each fully held regional objective. Subject-held land counts toward territorial completion, but Serbia makes the political settlement.
- Recognition of the current heir, with the benefit lost if that heir changes or a different person succeeds.
- A succession ledger, displayed strain calculation, monthly settlement progress, and centralized or negotiated imperial outcomes.
- A modest situation map: direct Serbian possessions and remaining objective territories. Subject-held territory is in the objective color even where it counts toward completion.

## Keeping the scope small

The regional borders are **game-province approximations**, not a location-perfect reconstruction of the empire in 1348. The upload did not include map definitions or country setup. Every province identifier used here was verified in the supplied 1.4.6 scripts.

| Objective | Game province definitions |
| --- | --- |
| Macedonia | Upper, Central, and Lower Macedonia |
| Albania | Albania, excluding the Durrës location |
| Epirus and Acarnania | North Epirus, South Epirus, and Aetolia |
| Thessaly | Thessaly |

Thessaloniki is outside the required objective group. Neither Thessaloniki nor Durrës is required for completion. A province conquest casus belli can still allow a peace treaty covering more land than the historical objective; this prototype does not add a custom restricted peace system.

Named magnate families, detailed secession wars, Constantinople ambitions, church reconciliation, custom government laws, and fine border corrections are deferred. Existing unrelated Serbian events, including mining, Palman, the capital event, and later cultural content, remain unchanged and continue to use their vanilla conditions. They are not all new situation objectives.

## Political settlements

Each held objective can be settled once:

- **Royal administration:** six months of income, 5 stability, and 0.05 noble satisfaction. Creates no continuing delegation burden.
- **Delegated governorship:** grants 0.05 noble satisfaction, adds 8 succession strain, and applies a lasting 2.5% tax-efficiency penalty. This is an abstract commitment, not an independent subject or character office.

The successor may reform a delegated governorship for six months of income, 10 government power, 5 stability, and 0.1 noble satisfaction. The regional territory must still be held. Concessions survive territorial loss and remain listed in the ledger.

Recognition of an heir costs the assembly price: two months of income and 5 government power. Negotiating the compact has the same assembly price and is available after the first year of the inheritance.

Coronation and Code deliberation each offer their existing event once. Declining is a real choice in this first build; there is no repeatable prestige-farming ceremony or repeat Code decision.

## Succession strain and endings

Strain is recalculated monthly and after situation actions. It is a transparent score derived from commitments and ordinary game conditions, not a replacement estate system.

| Condition | Strain change |
| --- | ---: |
| Base | 40 |
| Each delegated governorship | +8 |
| Each fully held objective without a settlement | +8 |
| Code in force | -10 |
| Recognized successor | -15 |
| Negotiated compact | -15 |
| Stability below 20 | +10 |
| Legitimacy below 60 | +10 |
| Average home-region control below 0.5 | +10 |
| At war | +10 |
| Stability at least 50 | -5 |
| Legitimacy at least 80 | -5 |

The result is clamped to 0–100. An unprepared succession also costs 10 legitimacy and 10 stability at the opening event.

Both imperial endings require 36 **consecutive** settled months: peace, stability at least 20, legitimacy at least 60, strain at most 40, and a political settlement for every fully held regional objective. Falling short resets that consecutive count. Neither ending requires conquering all four regions.

- **The Consolidated Empire:** imperial rank, Code in force, no delegated governorships, no negotiated compact, and strain at most 25. Use the confirmation action. Grants 10 legitimacy, 10 prestige, and a 25-year modifier improving legitimacy and monthly control.
- **The Enduring Imperial Compact:** imperial rank, a negotiated compact, and strain at most 40. Use the ratification action. Grants 10 legitimacy, 10 stability, and a 25-year legitimacy modifier. The compact's 5% tax-efficiency concession and regional concessions remain in force.
- **An Unsettled Inheritance:** if neither settlement is confirmed within 240 months, the situation ends with a legitimacy penalty and a 15-year legitimacy modifier. This build does not automatically remove territories or force a civil war.

A smaller empire can therefore consolidate successfully. Remaining a kingdom is also possible, but does not earn an imperial victory in this first version.

## Vanilla integration and compatibility

Two vanilla files are overridden, using the supplied 1.4.6 versions:

- `in_game/events/DHE/flavor_ser.txt`: events `.30`, `.31`, and `.50` are presented through situation actions. Events `.10`, `.11`, `.12`, and `.260` are guarded against firing for a country managed by the mod. All other Serbian event definitions are unchanged.
- `in_game/common/disasters/curse_of_stefan_uros_iii.txt`: prevented from starting for mod-managed Serbia, so the same inheritance is not punished twice.

The `ros_managed` marker remains after the situations end, preventing the replaced collapse chain from returning after an imperial settlement. Generic game crises, wars, and rebellions remain possible.

This full-file event override is intentionally straightforward for the first build. Other mods changing the same files need a compatibility patch. Update these overrides against the game files when moving beyond 1.4.6. `REFERENCE_HASHES.json` records the exact input versions used.

## First in-game test

1. Confirm the first situation appears and its cards and actions have readable English text.
2. Open the conquest programme; inspect its CB targets and duration.
3. Verify that Macedonia can unlock coronation while Thessaloniki remains outside Serbia.
4. Open the coronation and Code events; confirm the original rewards, patriarchate, costs, and policy still work.
5. Complete a regional objective and try a royal settlement, then a delegated settlement in another region. Check the ledger and country modifier.
6. Prepare the heir. On a test save, check both a prepared and an unprepared succession. The second situation should appear after Dušan ceases to rule, without the old Serbian disaster firing too.
7. Check monthly strain and the settled-month counter. War or insufficient stability should reset the consecutive count.
8. Test each imperial ending on separate saves, including a smaller empire that does not hold all four objectives.

If the mod fails to load, the panel is blank, an action is missing, or an event has an error, send the game's `error.log` from the EU5 user `logs` directory and a screenshot of the relevant panel. The engine's parser and actual UI render are the next validation step.

## Development files and validation

`devtools/build_mod.py` is the generator and `devtools/validate_mod.py` performs static checks and evaluates the generated monthly effects and ending conditions with representative campaign states. To reproduce the build, place this package at `deliverables/rise_of_serbia` beneath a work directory and extract the original reference archive into that directory's `eu5_reference` folder, then run the two scripts.

The checks cover script/GUI structure, localization references, unchanged unrelated Serbian events, crisis guards, reachable endings, monthly counter resets, and 400 combinations of political conditions. They **do not** substitute for the game engine. See `VALIDATION.json`.

Technical reference: the uploaded EU5 1.4.6 scripts, especially `common/situations/readme.txt`, `gui/panels/situation/readme.txt`, the Ottoman situation and actions, and Serbia's historical events. Folder and metadata conventions were cross-checked against the [EU5 community toolkit](https://github.com/Europa-Universalis-5-Modding-Co-op/community-mod-toolkit).
